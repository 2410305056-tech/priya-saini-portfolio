## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the calculated precision, intellectual gravitas, and quiet authority of modern forensic science and laboratory research. Built specifically for an editorial portfolio, it treats every case study, publication, and instrumentation report as a cataloged forensic dossier or peer-reviewed monograph.

### Aesthetic Persona & Movements
- **Clinical Editorial & Specimen Indexing:** Blends high-end scientific publishing with rigorous archival documentation. It uses hairline rules, specimen tagging grids, case accession labels, and structured data blocks.
- **Sterile Precision:** Uncompromisingly clean and analytical. It excludes all sensationalist, theatrical, or graphic tropes often associated with forensics in popular media, focusing strictly on objective inquiry, chemical spectroscopy, digital chain of custody, and peer-reviewed rigor.
- **Target Audience:** Academic search committees, clinical pathology directors, criminal justice institutions, research fellowships, and legal forensic partners seeking verifiable expertise.

## Colors
The color palette establishes an authentic, high-clarity laboratory environment. The system balances deep institutional navies with crisp, sterilized surfaces and controlled instrumentation accents.

- **Primary (`#0A192F`):** Deep Obsidian Navy. Used for core structural boundaries, authoritative headlines, primary badges, and high-emphasis data states. Evokes institutional trust and legal permanence.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** Precision Spectrometry Blue. Applied sparingly for active calibration points, interactive states, highlighted data markers, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#475569`):** Slate Archive. Dedicated to metadata values, structural hairline grids, secondary analytical labels, and supporting contextual notes.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`):** Calibrated Vitreous White. High-reflectance, glare-free background base simulating laboratory document stock and cleanroom surfaces.
- **Border & Grid Tone (`#E2E8F0`):** Hairline Index Rule. Establishes micro-grids and strict spatial containers without visual clutter.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy unites editorial scholarship with technical recording:

- **Headlines (Source Serif 4):** Anchors case names, research monographs, and chapter divisions with the prestige of medical-legal publications.
- **Body & Running Text (Inter):** Maximizes optical clarity and objective readability in multi-column research breakdowns, methodology descriptions, and portfolio abstracts.
- **Archival Tags & Metadata (JetBrains Mono):** Drives the specimen indexing system. Applied strictly to chain-of-custody timestamps, specimen serial IDs, chemical compound indicators, and equipment readouts. Always set with uppercase tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined micro-grid modeled on lab specimen accession logs and broadsheet academic reviews.

### Layout Model
- **Deskstop:** 12-column modular grid with a strict 72px baseline, bounded by a fixed maximum container width of `1440px`. Gutters are maintained at `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with consistent `1.5rem` gutters and adaptive margins of `2rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen edge margins. Multi-column metric panels collapse vertically into sequential specimen strips.

### Spatial Rhythms
- Sub-components observe an 8px rhythmic increment (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.75rem`, `3rem`).
- Spacing is defined by structural alignment against visible or implied hairline boundaries rather than buoyant, floating whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through planar stratification and calibrated micro-borders rather than ambient drop shadows.

- **Low-Contrast Hairline Grid:** Surfaces rely on 1px solid borders (`#E2E8F0`) to establish hierarchy. Elements do not lift off the page; they exist within a single flat plane subdivided into sterile fields.
- **Tonal Tiers:** Recessed metadata bays, instrumentation readouts, and table cells utilize subtle shifts in background tint:
  - Surface Zero (Canvas): `#F8FAFC`
  - Tier 1 (Specimen Chamber / Cards): `#FFFFFF`
  - Tier 2 (Analytical Wells / Monospaced Data Rows): `#F1F5F9`
- **Elevation Accent:** Hover and selection states use an active border transition to `#0284C7` (1px) combined with an immediate flat background shift, avoiding decorative shadows to preserve scientific austerity.

## Shapes
The design uses an architectural, non-rounded shape language (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Edges (0px):** All containers, data cells, buttons, imagery viewports, and tag badges feature crisp, unrounded right angles. 
- **Analytical Framing:** The strict orthogonal profile directly mirrors microscopy slides, test well cassettes, slide mounts, and forensic logbooks.
- **Corner Notches & Precision Ticks:** Interactive or indexable components occasionally utilize micro 4px corner tick-marks or hairline registration crosses along their bounding borders to evoke microscope reticles and calibrated glass plates.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0A192F` fill, sharp corners (0px), text in `label-md` (`JetBrains Mono`, all caps, tracking 0.08em) in `#FFFFFF`. Hover state: background shifts to `#0284C7` with no motion or scale change.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1px hairline border (`#0A192F`), transparent background, `#0A192F` text. Hover state: fills `#F1F5F9` while retaining border integrity.
- **Indexing Trigger:** Monospaced prefix indicators (e.g., `[ REF-01 -> ]`) that shift 2px horizontally on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specimen Tags:** Height of 22px, sharp corners, `#F1F5F9` background, 1px border in `#CBD5E1`. Font: `label-sm` in `#334155`.
- **Status Indicators (e.g., "ARCHIVED", "PUBLISHED", "CHAIN-VERIFIED"):** Prefixed with a 4px solid square pip in `#0284C7` or `#475569`, followed by uppercase monospaced text.

### Dossier Cards (Case Studies & Research)
- Background `#FFFFFF` with 1px border `#E2E8F0`.
- Top edge features an accession band: a 24px tall utility strip in `#F1F5F9` containing the specimen log number (`JetBrains Mono`) and date stamp separated by a vertical hairline.
- Content zone contains editorial serif titles (`headline-sm`) followed by concise abstract copy (`body-sm`).
- Hover state: The outer border transitions from `#E2E8F0` to `#0284C7` with no drop shadow.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Rectangular field with 1px border in `#CBD5E1`, `#FFFFFF` interior.
- Placeholder text styled in `body-sm` with color `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Replaces border with a crisp 1px `#0284C7` rule with an optional micro-label anchored directly into the top border segment.

### Lists & Specimen Tables
- Alternating or zebra-striped rows are avoided. Instead, rows are partitioned by continuous 1px `#E2E8F0` horizontal divider lines.
- Columns align explicitly to fixed character counts using `JetBrains Mono` for comparative numerical data (e.g., retention times, sample weights, variance metrics).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkbox:** Crisp 14x14px square box, 1px border in `#334155`. Checked state displays a solid 8x8px square centered fill in `#0A192F`.
- **Radio Button:** 14x14px square outer container with a 6x6px inner square fill upon selection, retaining consistent right-angled geometry across all controls.
